# AgriNexus (KrishiMitra) — SIH Showcase Video Script
**Runtime:** ~10 minutes (≈1,400 words at 140 wpm) · **Speakers:** split sections across 4–6 team members · **Format:** narration + screen recordings

> Delivery tips: speak the quoted lines slower and with a pause after them. Keep demo clips 8–15 s each. Lower music during Hindi phrases.

---

## [0:00 – 0:50] COLD OPEN — The Hook
*[ON SCREEN: Drone shot over a green paddy field at dawn. Slow zoom on a farmer walking into the field.]*

Six in the morning. Somewhere in Uttar Pradesh, a farmer walks into his field — the same field his father walked, and his grandfather before him. He can read the sky better than any weather app. He can smell rain before the clouds gather.

But when disease strikes his crop... when the water runs short... when a trader quotes him half the fair price... he stands alone.

This is Ramesh's story. It is the story of more than 140 million Indian farm holdings. And it is the story we decided to rewrite.

*[TITLE CARD: **AgriNexus — KrishiMitra.** Tagline: "From soil to sale, from sowing to subsidy."]*

---

## [0:50 – 2:10] THE PROBLEM
*[ON SCREEN: Split screen — diseased leaf / dry cracked soil / mandi yard / paperwork stack.]*

Here is the hard truth. India loses an estimated ₹1.5 lakh crore of agricultural produce every year to pests, disease and mismanaged water. The average farmer captures only a fraction of the price a consumer pays — the middleman takes the rest. And government schemes worth thousands of crores — income support, crop insurance, credit — fail to reach the very farmers they were built for, because eligibility is confusing, paperwork is intimidating, and information travels slowly.

Four problems. One farmer. No single place to turn to.

Until now.

---

## [2:10 – 2:55] THE SOLUTION
*[ON SCREEN: Logo animation, then the landing page with live weather widget and scrolling mandi ticker.]*

Meet **AgriNexus — KrishiMitra**. A farmer's digital companion that walks with him from soil to sale, from sowing to subsidy. One platform, three sides — farmer, merchant, customer. Eight Indian languages. On the web and on Android. And judges, you can explore the whole product without even signing up — our showcase mode opens the experience to everyone.

Built on one non-negotiable principle: *every advisory must be scientifically defensible.* We don't guess. We compute.

---

## [2:55 – 3:40] CROP DOCTOR — AI Plant Pathologist
*[ON SCREEN: Close-up of a diseased paddy leaf → camera capture → diagnosis result screen.]*

Watch what happens when Ramesh spots yellow spots on his paddy. He opens KrishiMitra, taps the camera — one photo. Within seconds, our Gemini-powered **Crop Doctor** identifies the disease, grades its severity from low to critical, explains the cause, and prescribes both organic *and* chemical remedies — in his own language. Every analysis is saved to his crop history, so he can track recovery over time.

No waiting for an extension officer. No trial-and-error spraying.

**A plant pathologist in every pocket.**

---

## [3:40 – 5:00] DIGITAL TWIN — The Scientific Core
*[ON SCREEN: Tap-to-draw field boundary on Esri satellite imagery → area readout → soil moisture gauge at 3 depths → advisory card.]*

But diagnosis is only half the battle. Farming is decided by water and weather. So we built the **Digital Twin** — a living, scientific mirror of Ramesh's farm.

He draws his farm boundary with a fingertip, directly on satellite imagery. The system instantly measures the area — in acres, hectares, even *guntha*. Then real physics takes over: satellite-derived soil moisture at three depths, live weather, and the **FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration model** — the same international standard used by agronomists worldwide — feed a day-by-day water balance of his field.

Out comes advice he can trust: when to irrigate, how much, expected yield, and fertilizer guidance. No black boxes — **every number can be traced to a published scientific method.**

And when crop damage strikes, one tap exports a **PMFBY-ready evidence card** — crop, damage percentage, date range, location. Printable proof that makes crop-insurance claims faster and fairer.

---

## [5:00 – 5:45] WHAT-IF SIMULATOR — Rehearsing the Weather
*[ON SCREEN: Simulator sliders being dragged — temperature, dry spell, deluge, irrigation — twin charts reacting day by day.]*

Now — what if the rains fail? What if a heatwave hits?

Our **What-If Simulator** lets a farmer drag four sliders — temperature deviation, dry-spell length, excess deluge rainfall, irrigation supply — and watch his *own* farm's future unfold over the next 30 days. The twin recalculates soil moisture, crop stress and yield impact, then recommends exactly what to do: irrigate now, mulch, drain the field, delay urea.

Farmers stop being spectators to the weather — **they rehearse for it.**

---

## [5:45 – 6:30] CONSENSUS ENGINE + AGRONOMY RAG — Honest AI
*[ON SCREEN: Multi-agent debate visualisation → final consensus card with reasoning shown.]*

Behind the scenes, something even more interesting. Ask KrishiMitra a hard agronomy question, and multiple independent AI agents analyse it separately, debate their disagreements, and return a **consensus answer** — with the reasoning shown.

Our **Agronomy-RAG** engine grounds every answer in a curated knowledge base using vector search, so advice comes from verified agricultural science — not internet rumours. If the science is divided, the farmer sees that too.

**Honest AI. That was a design decision, not an afterthought.**

---

## [6:30 – 7:00] WEATHER, VOICE & LEARN
*[ON SCREEN: 7-day harvest window strip → voice query being spoken → spoken answer → Learn page guides.]*

A 7-day **harvest window** tells Ramesh the best day to cut his crop — optimal, good, caution, or avoid. **Voice input and spoken answers** mean a farmer who cannot read can still use everything. And a Learn library — paddy blast, neem pest control, blight management — knowledge that once travelled by word of mouth now travels at the speed of light.

---

## [7:00 – 7:55] MARKETPLACE — Three Sides, One Fair Price
*[ON SCREEN: Farmer listing produce with photos → merchant shop with inventory & orders → customer checkout → mandi price ticker → price ladder graphic.]*

Growing the crop is half the battle. Selling it is the other half. So AgriNexus is a full **three-sided marketplace**. Farmers list produce with photos. Merchants run real shops — inventory, orders, dashboards. Customers buy direct. Live **mandi prices** scroll on the home screen, and transparent **price ladders** show what each grade should fetch — so no trader can lowball a farmer again.

Our **pooled escrow pre-orders** let buyer groups commit to a harvest before it's even sown — the farmer gets a guaranteed buyer, buyers get traceable produce. And **Farm-to-Product** guidance helps farmers grade, process and package their harvest into higher-value goods.

**The middleman's monopoly just met its match.**

---

## [7:55 – 8:55] GOVERNMENT SCHEMES — Closing the Delivery Gap
*[ON SCREEN: /schemes page → matched schemes with "You qualify" reasons → document checklist → Gap Insights dashboard chart.]*

Now, the invisible problem. India doesn't have a scheme shortage — it has a delivery gap. So we built a **Government Schemes engine**. A plain TypeScript rules engine — no AI hand-waving — matches each farmer's profile against the *real* eligibility criteria of PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Kisan Credit Card, PM-KISAN Maandhan, Soil Health Card, and state schemes like UP's Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana.

It doesn't just say "you qualify" — it says **why**, in plain language. Then a **document-readiness checklist**: Aadhaar linked? Bank account? Land record? Missing items link straight to the fix.

And one step further — a **Gap Insights dashboard** shows what percentage of *eligible* farmers on our platform report never receiving benefits. Plus a one-tap "applied, no response" report. Not to point fingers — to make the gap visible. Because **what is measured gets fixed.**
